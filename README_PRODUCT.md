## The All-in-One School Mobile Platform

Sawaed Elm is a comprehensive mobile platform designed for modern schools. It brings students, parents, teachers, administrators, and support staff into a single connected experience — available on Android, iOS, and Web.

From daily attendance and assignments to real-time bus tracking and AI-powered tutoring, Sawaed Elm replaces scattered tools and manual processes with one unified, beautifully designed app that every member of the school community can use with confidence.

---

## Built for Every Member of the School Community

### For Students
Stay on top of every assignment, exam, and deadline. Chat with classmates, submit work directly from your phone, check your bus status, and get instant help from **Sawaed** — your personal AI study assistant — available anytime, in your own language.

### For Parents
Always know what's happening with your child. View attendance records the moment they are marked, track bus location and drop-off status, review payment plans, and follow your child's academic progress — all from one app. Managing multiple children? Switch between them with a single tap.

### For Teachers
Manage your entire workload from your phone. Create assignments and exams targeted to individual students, sections, or full grades. Mark attendance with one tap, review submissions, assign grades, and communicate directly with students and parents — without switching between apps.

### For School Administrators
Full visibility and control over every aspect of school operations. Manage users and roles, approve registrations, oversee finance and payments, monitor transportation, and broadcast announcements — all with fine-grained permission control so every staff member sees exactly what they need.

### For Drivers and Transport Staff
A clear, organised journey screen shows every student in drop-off order, highlights absent students instantly, and lets you notify families the moment their child steps off the bus. Start journeys, record status updates, and send urgent alerts — all from the driver's dedicated view.

## Core Features

### Academic Management
- **Assignments & Exams** — Create homework, assignments, and exams targeted to individual students, sections, or full grades. Set deadlines, receive submissions, and give feedback with grades — all in one flow.
- **Week Plans** — Teachers can publish structured weekly lesson plans aligned to the school calendar and working days.
- **Auto-Close Control** — Toggle whether assignments automatically close at the deadline or remain open until manually closed by the teacher.
- **File Submissions** — Students submit text answers, files, or external links. Supported file size is configurable by school.

---

### Smart Social Feed
- **Posts, Reels & Polls** — Share updates, short video reels, and interactive polls with the school community. Target content by role or open it to everyone.
- **User Statuses** — Post a 24-hour status that your school community can follow in real time.
- **Reactions & Comments** — React with emoji, comment, and reply — familiar social interactions in a safe, school-controlled environment.
- **Privacy by Design** — All content is scoped within the school. Nothing is visible outside the platform.

---

### Direct Messaging
- **One-to-One Chat** — Private messages between any two users in the school. Teachers message parents, students message teachers, drivers message staff — directly and securely.
- **Smart Notifications** — If a message is unread for more than 5 minutes, an automatic reminder is sent to ensure nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Silent Alerts** — When the app is open but the chat is in the background, a silent notification updates the badge count without interrupting the user.

---

### Real-Time Notifications
- **Instant Delivery** — Push notifications reach the right people the moment something happens: a student is marked absent, an assignment is published, a payment is overdue, or a bus journey starts.
- **Smart Scheduling** — Exam reminders go out 3 days, 2 days, and 24 hours before. Assignment reminders follow a 24h → 12h → 1h countdown.
- **Targeted Broadcasts** — Send notifications to specific roles, grades, grade sections, or individual users.
- **Notification History** — Every user has a personal notification inbox with read/unread tracking and category filters.

---

### Attendance & Leave Management
- **One-Tap Marking** — Teachers mark attendance for their class in seconds. The system auto-marks holidays and approved leave days automatically.
- **Instant Parent Alerts** — Parents and authorised staff are notified immediately when a student is marked absent or late.
- **Leave Requests** — Students and employees submit leave requests through the app. Reviewers approve or reject with a single tap, and the calendar and attendance records update automatically.
- **Attendance Reports** — Detailed per-student reports with summary statistics, attendance rate percentages, and CSV/PDF export.

---

### Finance & Payment Tracking
- **Payment Plans** — Schools configure yearly, term-based, or monthly instalment plans with custom amounts, due dates, and currencies.
- **Instalment Tracking** — Every payment is recorded manually by authorised staff. Parents see real-time payment status for their children — paid, pending, partially paid, or overdue.
- **Automatic Reminders** — Reminder notifications are sent to parents automatically before each instalment due date (configurable in advance).
- **Journey Fee Plans** — Transportation fees are managed through the same system as school fees, with separate tracking per category.
- **Finance Reports** — School-wide collection summaries, per-student sorted reports, overdue lists, and bulk reminder tools — all with permission-gated access.

---

### Transportation Management
- **Bus Fleet Management** — Manage the school's full bus fleet with bus numbers, vehicle IDs, and driver assignments.
- **Journey Planning** — Create pick-up and drop-off journeys, assign buses, drivers, and monitoring staff, and add students from any grade.
- **Recurring Journeys** — Set journeys to repeat daily, on working days only, weekly, or on specific dates — no manual entry required.
- **Driver App Experience** — Drivers see students in their arranged drop-off order, with absent students visually highlighted. Mark students as boarded, dropped off, or not boarded with a single tap.
- **Family Notifications** — Parents receive a push notification the moment their child is dropped off. If a student does not board, the parent and school staff are notified immediately.
- **Urgent Alerts** — Drivers can broadcast an emergency alert to all affected families and staff instantly — for breakdowns, accidents, or unexpected delays.
- **Bus Info for Families** — Students and parents see the bus number, driver name, mobile number, and journey status directly in the app.

---

### School Calendar & Holidays
- **Smart Weekend Configuration** — Schools configure which days of the week are weekends (e.g., Friday–Saturday or Saturday–Sunday). The system applies this across the entire academic year automatically.
- **Holiday Management** — Add public holidays and school closures one by one, or define a recurring rule (e.g., "mark every Friday as a public holiday for the 2025–2026 year") and let the system generate all the dates.
- **School Events** — Create and publish school events — sports days, open days, exam weeks — visible to selected roles or the full school community.
- **Integrated Calendar** — Every user sees a personalised calendar combining their assignments, exams, approved leave, journey events, holidays, and school events in one view.

---

### Sawaed — Your AI Study Companion

Meet **Sawaed** (سواعد), the platform's built-in AI assistant. Sawaed is not a generic chatbot — it is a personalised learning companion that knows who it is talking to.

- **Personalised from the first message** — Sawaed is given the user's name, age, grade, preferred language, and family context before the conversation even begins. A parent sees a helpful co-parenting assistant; a student sees a patient tutor; a teacher sees a knowledgeable colleague.
- **Speaks your language** — Sawaed responds in Arabic, English, or French, in a tone and vocabulary matched to the user's age and role.
- **Voice support** — Users can speak to Sawaed and listen to its responses read aloud, making it accessible for young students and users on the go.
- **App guide built in** — Sawaed knows how to use the app. Ask "how do I submit my assignment?" or "where can I see my child's attendance?" and get an instant, accurate answer.
- **Full conversation history** — Every session is saved. Users can browse past conversations, start fresh sessions, and pick up where they left off.
- **Always free** — Sawaed is powered by permanently free AI models, ensuring no hidden costs for schools.

---

### User & Role Management
- **Fully Dynamic Roles** — There are no fixed system roles. Schools define their own roles (Teacher, Supervisor, Driver, Finance Officer, etc.) and assign any combination of permissions to each.
- **Granular Permissions** — Over 60 specific permission codes control exactly what each user can see and do — from viewing only student records, to approving leave, to managing the entire school.
- **Per-User Overrides** — Individual permissions can be granted to any user on top of their role, for flexible edge cases without creating a new role.
- **Registration Approval Flow** — New users register through social login (Google / Apple) and are placed in a pending queue. Administrators review and approve or reject each registration with one tap.
- **Employee + Parent in One Account** — A teacher who is also a parent of an enrolled student needs only one account. Their role governs school operations access; their family links govern parental access. No duplicate accounts, no confusion.

---

### Dashboard & Insights
- **Personalised Dashboard** — Every user sees a dashboard tailored to their role and permissions. Students see assignments and their calendar. Parents see their children's performance and payments. Admins see school-wide statistics.
- **Student Performance Charts** — Grade trends, assignment completion rates, and subject-by-subject analysis visualised clearly for students, parents, and academic supervisors.
- **Finance Overview Charts** — School-wide collection rate, monthly collection trends, and overdue breakdowns — all in real time for authorised finance staff.
- **Multi-Child View** — Parents with more than one enrolled child switch between children with one tap, with all dashboards updating instantly.

---

### Security & Privacy
- **School-Scoped Data** — Every piece of data is strictly isolated to the school it belongs to. No data is ever shared across schools.
- **Social Login Only** — Authentication is handled entirely through Google and Apple — no passwords to store, no credential leaks.
- **End-to-End Audit Trail** — Every create, update, and delete action is recorded in an immutable audit log with full before/after snapshots. Nothing is hidden.
- **Soft Delete Everywhere** — Data is never permanently erased. Deleted records are retained for compliance and historical accuracy.
- **Encryption** — All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Force Update & Maintenance Mode** — Schools can require users to update to the latest app version and enable maintenance mode with a custom message — no code release required.
